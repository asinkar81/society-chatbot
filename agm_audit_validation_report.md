## 1. Accounting Policy on Member-Incurred Expenses (Plots 13 & 26)

### Principle: Expense Reimbursement vs. Maintenance Credit
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Proper Society Accounting Practice**: Under standard cooperative society accounting principles, **operating expenses incurred by members should NEVER be directly credited against maintenance dues in member ledgers**.

* **Why Offsetting in Member Ledgers is Flawed**:
  * Offsetting creates artificial distortions in the member's maintenance register, bypasses expense approval vouchers, and obscures true annual repair expenditures in the society's income/expenditure account.
* **The Correct Procedure**:
  1. The member pays their society maintenance charges **in full**.
  2. The member submits original bills/vouchers for the expenditure incurred on behalf of the society.
  3. The society issues a **formal direct reimbursement** (via cheque or bank transfer) to the member, debiting `Repairs & Maintenance Account`.
* **Application to Identified Entries**:
  * **Plot 13 (Mr. Rishikesh Phutane)**: Accountant credited ₹1,000 on 03-Apr-2024 via non-cash Journal #45 for *"REPAIR & MAINTENANCE AT SITE WORK FOR ELECTRIC (old Expenses)"*.
    * *Resolution*: The society will issue a direct payment/reimbursement of ₹1,000 to Mr. Phutane. His maintenance ledger will reflect standard maintenance demand and dues.
  * **Plot 26 (Mr. Ashutosh Sinkar)**: ₹140 petty cash entry in Oct 2023.
    * *Resolution*: Reimbursed directly from petty cash; member ledger kept strictly for maintenance contributions.
